      DTL (Vakaros): the boat's own device figure from the feed, bow-corrected; the primary number. Check: our estimate from the GPS track (bow offset along heading, at the device's start fix ~0.9 s before the gun). Late: DTL ÷ speed toward the line. Line pos: 0% pin, 100% RC.

      TWA / VMG are estimates. The boats carry no wind sensor; wind direction comes from the fleet's steady upwind tack headings (cross-checked against a weather model). VMG = distance made good along the wind ÷ leg time. Leg TWA = median |TWA| while sailing steadily. ▲ best / ▼ worst in column.

      Unofficial. Data: public Vakaros RaceSense feeds (race control + 5 Hz GPS), fetched per race and cached. Not affiliated with Vakaros. A Walke Forward project.
