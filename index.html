    Tracks by leg

    GPS tracker tracks (bow offset is a few metres and omitted at this scale). Legs split at each boat's closest approach to the mark trackers. Pick a leg above to show only it. Colour: Rank shades each boat by its elapsed-time rank (yellow and boldest fastest, fading to faint purple; Highlight greys all but the top 3 or 10, and a picked boat outside them is drawn in white (dark by day) with its rank; hover a track for its time). Colour: Boat speed shades every stretch of track by that boat's speed over ground at the time (4 s average; dark purple slow, through teal, to yellow-green fast; grey dashed = telemetry gap; hover a track for the boat, time, speed and leg). For the whole race, Scale: Per leg stretches the colours over each leg's own spread of fleet speeds, so beats and runs both show who was quick; Whole race uses one knot range for every leg. Select a boat in the list, the start chart or a table row to highlight it.
